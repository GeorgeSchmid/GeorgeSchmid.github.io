Short-Term Rental Expenses: The Complete Checklist

Every cost to track for an Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo rental, grouped by how often you pay it. Missing even a few makes your rental look more profitable than it is.

Sort your costs into two kinds. Per-stay costs grow with every check-in, so short stays cost more. Fixed costs stay the same whether you're full or empty, so they decide your break-even point. The nightly rate calculator uses both to show your minimum price.

Every stay

  • Cleaning (your cleaner or your own time)
  • Laundry: sheets and towels
  • Toiletries: soap, shampoo, toilet paper
  • Kitchen basics: coffee, tea, oil, salt, dish soap
  • Welcome items
  • Key handover or lockbox codes, if you pay for them

Every month

  • Rent or mortgage payment
  • Electricity, water, heating or gas
  • Internet and TV
  • Channel manager, pricing or messaging software
  • Building or HOA fees
  • Co-host or property manager fee

Every year

  • Insurance (spread over 12 months)
  • Property tax
  • Short-term rental licence or registration
  • Accountant or tax filing costs
  • Safety checks: fire extinguisher, smoke and CO alarms

Now and then

  • Repairs: plumber, electrician, appliance fixes
  • Replacing towels, bedding, pillows and mattress protectors
  • Broken or worn items: glasses, pans, kettle
  • Painting and small renovations
  • Professional photos for the listing
  • Furniture and decor updates

Easy to forget

  • Payment or currency-conversion fees
  • Guest damage that isn't reimbursed
  • Your own travel to the property
  • Refunds and discounts
  • Tourist tax you collect but still have to pay over

How to track them without the headache

  • Keep one list for all properties, with a column for which property each cost belongs to. Leave it empty for shared costs.
  • Log costs weekly. It takes five minutes and nothing gets lost.
  • Spread annual bills across 12 months when you look at monthly profit.
  • Keep receipts. What counts as deductible for tax depends on your country, so check with a local accountant.

Then plug your totals into the Airbnb profit calculator to see what's left.
