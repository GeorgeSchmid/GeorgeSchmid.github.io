How it works

  1. Set your currency, properties and each platform's fee once.
  2. Type each booking: dates, nightly rate and cleaning fee. Nights, revenue, fee and payout calculate automatically.
  3. Log expenses as they happen.
  4. Open the dashboard to see profit, occupancy and average rate for every month.

Why track profit, not payouts

A month with a big payout can still lose money once cleaning, laundry, utilities and rent or mortgage are paid. Tracking monthly profit shows which months carry the year, where costs creep up, and whether a price change helped. Try a single month for free with the Airbnb profit calculator.
