What a good owner statement shows

Owners want to see three things: what the property earned, what was taken off and why, and what they receive. A clear statement lists every booking, the platform fees, your management commission, any expenses you paid on their behalf, and the final owner payout. Sending the same format every month builds trust and saves questions.

Agree the commission base

Commission can be charged on the net payout, on booking revenue, or on booking revenue including cleaning fees. These give different amounts, so agree it in writing and pick the same option in the template.
