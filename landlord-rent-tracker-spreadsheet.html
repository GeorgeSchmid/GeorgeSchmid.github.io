What a rent roll is

A rent roll is a list of your units with the rent due and what each tenant has actually paid. Kept month by month, it shows arrears early, before a small gap becomes a big one, and gives you clean numbers for your accountant at the end of the year.

Built for private landlords

If you have between 1 and 20 long-term rentals, accounting software is often more than you need. This spreadsheet does the essentials: who paid, who owes, what each unit costs and earns.

Does it handle partial payments?

Yes. A month shows Partial when some rent was received, and the remaining amount is added to what's owed.

Is it suitable for tax?

It gives you rent received and expenses per unit, which your accountant can use. Tax rules differ by country.

Yes, there's a deposit column for every lease.
