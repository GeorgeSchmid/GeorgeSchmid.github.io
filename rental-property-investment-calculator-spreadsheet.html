The numbers that matter when buying a rental

Cash flow is what's left each month after the mortgage and all running costs. Cap rate compares net operating income with the price, ignoring the loan, so you can compare properties fairly. Cash-on-cash return shows what your own invested cash earns. For a short-term rental, break-even occupancy tells you how full the calendar must be just to cover costs: the lower, the safer the deal.

Airbnb or long-term rental?

Short-term renting usually earns more gross income but carries higher costs and more risk. Putting the same property in both columns shows whether the extra work actually pays.
