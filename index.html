Why payouts aren't profit

Booking platforms show you what they pay out. They don't show what's left after cleaning, laundry, utilities, supplies, repairs, insurance and your rent or mortgage. A month with a big payout can still lose money, and a quiet month can cost you more than you think.

The calculators on this site help you answer the questions that matter: how much profit you make, what nightly rate you need, how much the platform takes and how full your calendar really is.
