The formulas

Payout = (nightly rate × nights + cleaning fee) × (1 − fee %)
Price to charge = payout you want ÷ (1 − fee %)

Example

A 3-night stay at €100 a night with a €40 cleaning fee is a €340 booking. At a 15.5% host-only fee, the platform keeps €52.70 and you receive €287.30, or €95.77 per night.

A common mistake is to add 15.5% to your price to cover the fee. That isn't quite enough: to receive €300 you need to charge €355.03, not €346.50, because the fee is taken from the higher price too.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Airbnb take from hosts in 2026?

Most hosts are now on Airbnb's single host-only fee of about 15.5%, deducted from the payout. Airbnb moved hosts off the older split fee (about 3% for the host, with a separate fee charged to the guest) in stages through 2026. Your exact rate is shown on your payout report.

Does the platform fee apply to the cleaning fee?

On Airbnb's host-only fee, yes: the fee is calculated on the nightly rate plus cleaning fee and other host charges, excluding taxes. Booking.com commission also usually applies to the full room price including cleaning if it's part of the price.

How much commission does Booking.com charge?

Booking.com commission is typically around 15%, but it varies by country and by programme (for example Preferred Partner or Genius). Check your extranet or monthly invoice for your exact rate.

How do I price so I get the payout I want?

Divide the payout you want by (1 − fee rate). For a 15.5% fee and a €300 payout: 300 ÷ 0.845 = €355.03. Use the “Price for target payout” tab above to do this automatically.
