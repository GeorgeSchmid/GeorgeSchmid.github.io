How it's calculated

Nightly rate = (fixed costs + cost per stay × stays − cleaning fee × stays × (1 − fee %) + profit goal) ÷ (nights × (1 − fee %))

Stays = nights ÷ average stay length. For the break-even rate, the profit goal is set to zero.

Example

With €900 of fixed costs, 20 booked nights in stays of 3 nights, €38 of cleaning and supplies per stay, a €40 cleaning fee and a 15.5% platform fee:

  • Break-even rate: €54.91 per night
  • Rate for a €500 monthly profit: €84.50 per night

In a quieter month with only 12 nights, the same costs push break-even up to about €90. Use this before the low season to decide whether to lower prices, allow shorter stays, or cut costs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set my nightly rate for a short-term rental?

Start from your costs, not from competitors: work out the rate that covers your fixed costs, the cost of each stay and the platform fee at the number of nights you realistically expect to book. That's your floor. Then compare with similar listings nearby and price above the floor wherever demand allows.

What is a break-even nightly rate?

It's the lowest average rate at which you make zero profit for the month. Charging less than this means every booking month loses money, unless you book many more nights.

Why does the rate change so much when I change the number of nights?

Fixed costs are shared across all booked nights. Spreading €900 over 10 nights adds €90 per night; over 25 nights it adds €36. That's why off-season months need either lower costs or more bookings at a lower rate.

Does this include the cleaning fee?

Yes. Enter the cleaning fee you charge guests; it offsets part of the cleaning cost, so the nightly rate you need goes down.
