Price from costs first, then from the market

Your break-even rate is the lowest average price at which a month covers all costs after platform fees. It changes every month because occupancy changes: quiet months need a higher rate per booked night to cover the same fixed costs. Knowing it stops you from discounting below cost. Then compare with what similar listings charge to see where you have room to go up.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace dynamic pricing tools?

No, it complements them. Use your break-even rate as the minimum price in your platform's smart pricing or pricing tool.

Which platform fees can I use?

Any. Enter your own fee percentage, for example about 15.5% for Airbnb's host-only fee or around 15% for Booking.com.

Does it handle weekend pricing?

Yes. Set a weekend uplift and it splits the goal rate into weekday and Friday/Saturday prices.
