Formulas

Occupancy = nights booked ÷ (days × units − blocked nights)
ADR = revenue ÷ nights booked  ·  RevPAR = revenue ÷ available nights

Example

21 nights booked in a 30-night month with €1,900 of revenue gives 70% occupancy, an ADR of €90.48 and a RevPAR of €63.33.

Why RevPAR beats occupancy alone

High occupancy can simply mean your price is too low, and a high nightly rate can hide an empty calendar. RevPAR rewards the right balance: if a price cut raises RevPAR, it earned you more money per available night. To see what that means for your profit after costs, use the Airbnb profit calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate occupancy rate for an Airbnb?

Divide the nights booked by the nights available in the period. Available nights are the days in the period times the number of units, minus nights you blocked yourself (for personal use or repairs).

What is ADR?

Average daily rate: your rental revenue divided by the number of nights booked. It shows what you earn on an average booked night.

What is RevPAR?

Revenue per available night (or room): revenue divided by all available nights, booked or not. It combines price and occupancy in one number, so it's the best single figure for comparing months or properties.

Should I include blocked nights?

Subtract nights you blocked on purpose, because they were never for sale. Don't subtract nights that simply didn't get booked; those are the ones occupancy is meant to measure.
